# deep-sea-ctf

## Community challenges

Signed-in players can use **Create** to submit a challenge with its description, flag, and solution write-up. New entries stay under review until the app owner approves them and assigns points in **Admin**. Approved challenges are published to **Library** and the board.

Expected flags are stored as keyed HMACs, not plaintext. A community challenge's write-up is visible to its author and the owner, and to other players only after they solve it.
